# ServerLens Product Context

register: product

## Users

ServerLens is for individual developers, small operations teams, and delivery engineers who need to understand the status of their own authorized servers without deploying a heavy observability platform.

## Product Purpose

The product provides a compact macOS-style control surface for server health, runtime, security, service ecosystem, and custom analysis reports.

## Delivery Principles

- Start with the operational workspace, not a marketing landing page.
- Every risk finding must include evidence and a concrete recommendation.
- Demo mode must work without external infrastructure so the app can be reviewed immediately.
- Security features are defensive only: analyze authorized telemetry, logs, ports, and service state.
- Keep the interface dense, calm, and suitable for repeated professional use.

## Anti-References

- No noisy cyberpunk security dashboard.
- No generic SaaS card mosaic.
- No decorative data visualizations that do not help operators decide.
- No unverified claims of network attack detection beyond evidence-backed rules.
